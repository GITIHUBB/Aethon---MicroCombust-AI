# NASA PHYSICAL SCIENCES INFORMATICS (PSI) INVESTIGATION RECORD
STUDY IDENTIFIER: PSI-159
TITLE: Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments / Cool Flames Investigation with Gases (ACME CFI-G)
PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR: Richard Axson (NASA GRC)
HARDWARE / FACILITY: Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR) / ACME
PROJECT TYPE: Microgravity Investigation (ISS)
TARGET MATERIALS: Propane, n-Butane, n-Pentane gas mixtures with up to 85% O2

## Executive Summary & Objectives
Map conditions for spherical cool diffusion flames and inside-out flame configurations for high-oxygen Moon Base environments.

## Experimental Approach & Diagnostics
Spherical porous burner gas injection in CIR chamber under microgravity.

## Benchmark Parameters & Telemetry Bounds
- Standard Atmospheric Pressure: 101.3 kPa (range 50 - 300 kPa)
- Oxygen Concentration Bounds: 15% to 30% O2 (up to 85% O2 in CFI-G)
- Airflow Velocity: 0 cm/s (quiescent) to 25 cm/s forced convective velocity
- Core Physics: Radiative Extinction, Quenching Limits, Flame Spread Rate, Soot Inception
