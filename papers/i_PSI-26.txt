# NASA PHYSICAL SCIENCES INFORMATICS (PSI) INVESTIGATION RECORD
STUDY IDENTIFIER: PSI-26
TITLE: Burning and Suppression of Solids (BASS)
PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR: Sandra Olson (NASA GRC)
HARDWARE / FACILITY: Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG) fan-driven flow duct
PROJECT TYPE: Microgravity Investigation (ISS)
TARGET MATERIALS: Cotton-fiberglass composite fabrics (75% cotton / 25% fiberglass), PMMA

## Executive Summary & Objectives
Bridge the gap between normal-gravity NASA-STD-6001 Test #1 flammability methods and actual microgravity solid flammability limits.

## Experimental Approach & Diagnostics
ISS microgravity flame spread testing with Kanthal wire igniter under variable forced airflow velocities (0 to 25 cm/s) and ambient oxygen levels.

## Benchmark Parameters & Telemetry Bounds
- Standard Atmospheric Pressure: 101.3 kPa (range 50 - 300 kPa)
- Oxygen Concentration Bounds: 15% to 30% O2 (up to 85% O2 in CFI-G)
- Airflow Velocity: 0 cm/s (quiescent) to 25 cm/s forced convective velocity
- Core Physics: Radiative Extinction, Quenching Limits, Flame Spread Rate, Soot Inception
