# NASA PHYSICAL SCIENCES INFORMATICS (PSI) INVESTIGATION RECORD
STUDY IDENTIFIER: PSI-98
TITLE: Spacecraft Fire Safety Experiment-I (SAFFIRE-I)
PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR: David Urban (NASA GRC) & Gary Ruff
HARDWARE / FACILITY: Cygnus Uncrewed Resupply Vehicle
PROJECT TYPE: Microgravity Flight Demonstration
TARGET MATERIALS: Sibal composite fabric (cotton/fiberglass sheet, 0.4 m wide x 1.0 m long)

## Executive Summary & Objectives
Measure large-scale flame spread speed and heat release rate in realistic spacecraft module volumes.

## Experimental Approach & Diagnostics
Automated ignition inside Cygnus cargo bay post-deorbit from ISS under forced 20 cm/s airflow.

## Benchmark Parameters & Telemetry Bounds
- Standard Atmospheric Pressure: 101.3 kPa (range 50 - 300 kPa)
- Oxygen Concentration Bounds: 15% to 30% O2 (up to 85% O2 in CFI-G)
- Airflow Velocity: 0 cm/s (quiescent) to 25 cm/s forced convective velocity
- Core Physics: Radiative Extinction, Quenching Limits, Flame Spread Rate, Soot Inception
