# NASA PHYSICAL SCIENCES INFORMATICS (PSI) INVESTIGATION RECORD
STUDY IDENTIFIER: PSI-107
TITLE: Smoke Point In Co-flow Experiment (SPICE)
PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR: David Urban (NASA GRC)
HARDWARE / FACILITY: Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG)
PROJECT TYPE: Microgravity Investigation (ISS)
TARGET MATERIALS: Methane, Ethylene, Gaseous Hydrocarbons

## Executive Summary & Objectives
Quantify smoke point transition limits and soot formation rates in microgravity diffusion flames.

## Experimental Approach & Diagnostics
Controlled fuel delivery with co-flow air duct aboard ISS Microgravity Glovebox.

## Benchmark Parameters & Telemetry Bounds
- Standard Atmospheric Pressure: 101.3 kPa (range 50 - 300 kPa)
- Oxygen Concentration Bounds: 15% to 30% O2 (up to 85% O2 in CFI-G)
- Airflow Velocity: 0 cm/s (quiescent) to 25 cm/s forced convective velocity
- Core Physics: Radiative Extinction, Quenching Limits, Flame Spread Rate, Soot Inception
