# NASA PHYSICAL SCIENCES INFORMATICS (PSI) INVESTIGATION RECORD
STUDY IDENTIFIER: PSI-62
TITLE: Concurrent Flame Spread Modeling using Flamelet Generated Manifolds in Microgravity with Comparison to BASS Experiments using Two-Color Tomography
PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR: Paul DesJardin (NYU Buffalo) & David Urban (NASA GRC)
HARDWARE / FACILITY: Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG) / BASS / BASS-II
PROJECT TYPE: Ground Investigation & Simulation Validation
TARGET MATERIALS: PMMA (Acrylic) flat sheets, spheres, rods, and wax cylinders

## Executive Summary & Objectives
Develop and validate Unsteady Flamelet Generated Manifold (UFGM) modeling techniques for concurrent flame spread in microgravity. Predict the 'Goldilocks' flammability zone.

## Experimental Approach & Diagnostics
Coupled fluid-solid responses with UFGM chemical kinetics reduction. Two-color pyrometry and DSLR tomography for 3D soot and temperature mapping.

## Benchmark Parameters & Telemetry Bounds
- Standard Atmospheric Pressure: 101.3 kPa (range 50 - 300 kPa)
- Oxygen Concentration Bounds: 15% to 30% O2 (up to 85% O2 in CFI-G)
- Airflow Velocity: 0 cm/s (quiescent) to 25 cm/s forced convective velocity
- Core Physics: Radiative Extinction, Quenching Limits, Flame Spread Rate, Soot Inception
